Apple Watch & Siri

Create Shortcuts on your iPhone that call the scoreboard. They also run from the Apple Watch (Shortcuts app, a watch-face complication, or the Action button) and from Siri. Your iPhone must be on the scoreboard's Wi-Fi.

  1. Open Shortcuts on iPhone, tap +, add the action Get Contents of URL.
  2. Set the URL to one of the addresses below, tap the arrow to show more options, and set Method to POST.
  3. Add the action Show Result (or Speak Text) so the watch shows the new score.
  4. Name it (e.g. “Point Team A”), open its details (ⓘ) and turn on Show on Apple Watch.

Wi-Fi

Changes take effect after a restart. If the network can't be joined, the board starts its own Wi-Fi instead.
